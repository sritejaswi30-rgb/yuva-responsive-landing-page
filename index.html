FAQ

Before you ask

Do I need prior experience?

No. The skill sprint is designed to place you — roughly a third of each cohort is starting from zero.

How much time per week?

Plan for 12–15 hours: two live sessions, three pod stand-ups and build time. Everything is recorded.

What does it cost?

The skill sprint is free. The full cohort has a pay-after-you're- hired option and 200 merit seats every batch.

Is it remote?

Fully async-friendly, with optional in-person demo days in 12 cities.
